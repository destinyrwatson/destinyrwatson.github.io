Personal Reflection

Career Journey

I started in retail and food service, building skills in customer service, teamwork, and technical tasks. These early roles taught me communication, responsibility, and adaptability.

Managing multiple responsibilities helped me learn problem solving, efficiency under pressure, and attention to detail. Each challenge strengthened my professional skills and readiness for growth.

Career Goals

Short term, I aim to gain experience through internships and hands on opportunities in Business Administration and IT. Long term, I hope to secure a professional role where I can contribute meaningfully and develop leadership skills.

Professional Philosophy

I strive to make a positive impact through my work by being detail oriented, proactive, and eager to learn. Challenges are opportunities to grow, and I value contributing to teams while advancing my own skills.
